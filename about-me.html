About PosNew Hub

PosNew Hub is more than a collection of links. It reflects a simple belief: efficiency is the foundation of great service.

While handling high-volume B2B clients in Batam, Ikhsan continually looks for ways to remove repetitive tasks. The portal acts as a unified command center for tools that support daily logistics and commercial workflows.

From HS Code lookups and Cost-Based Analysis calculators to shipment tracking and bulk data conversion, every workspace was created to address practical operational challenges and help deliver faster, smarter solutions.

PosNew Hub is an independently developed productivity portal. It is not an official corporate website of PT Pos Indonesia (Persero).
